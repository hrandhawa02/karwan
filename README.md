# Karwan

Karwan is a community record of showing up: a trailhead today, and later a concert or a place of worship. People mark that they went, and the page keeps what they found.

This repository starts with the **trailhead research page**. The phone app comes after the page has real reports.

## Research page

Hikers open one worldwide link on their phone. The map shows trailheads and parking lots already on OpenStreetMap for the area on screen. They pick one, or drop a pin, and answer only what the map does not already know.

The questions, the map rules, what we store, and how later reports may use the rows are in [docs/research-form.md](docs/research-form.md).

## Build order

1. Research page: map, search near the map, visit form, save each report.
2. A public summary for the area on screen, counts only.
3. iPhone app on the same stored reports.

No account, email, or fee on the research page. Nothing is written back to OpenStreetMap in this phase.
